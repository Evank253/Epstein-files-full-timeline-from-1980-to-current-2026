## 6. Matching standard and findings

A source-side debit is not, by itself, proof of a matched receiving-side credit. A matching account suffix or amount is not sufficient. A bank-recorded transfer description does not independently establish final settlement or beneficial ownership.

A transfer should be classified as **matched** only after obtaining and reconciling, at minimum:
1. the source-side transaction row and relevant statement context;
2. the receiving-side statement or bank transaction record showing the corresponding credit;
3. transaction date/value date, amount, currency, reference or trace identifier, and account identifiers;
4. reliable evidence identifying the receiving account holder at the transaction date; and
5. any reversals, returns, corrections, or intermediary movements necessary to explain differences.

**Current result:** No receiving-side credit has been established for the three February 2008 debit leads in EFTA01482909. No transfer in this update is classified as a confirmed debit/credit match.
