**What the report says:** The report describes Indyke as cashing checks below the $10,000 currency-transaction-reporting threshold; it quotes a bank email chain in which he reportedly discussed avoiding paperwork and cashing another check on a later day. It further states that the FBI record documented weekly $7,500 withdrawals on Epstein's behalf as of October 22, 2019. **Classification:** Congressional staff report summarizing cited records and deposition testimony. The underlying EFTA records and deposition transcript have not yet been acquired and page-image verified in this review.

**Limitations:** A transaction below $10,000 is not, by itself, proof of unlawful structuring. Intent, transaction aggregation, applicable reporting duties, account ownership, and the full contemporaneous context must be established. Do not convert the report's characterization into a criminal finding.
