## 1. Material source correction

The previous review treated the six records EFTA01482909, EFTA01482935, EFTA01482950, EFTA01482967, EFTA01483035 and EFTA01483039 as though each were a discrete DDA-to-DDA transfer instrument. That is not a safe description.

The independent report's table is a curated index of transfer-related records, but the source OCR shows at least some identifiers are pages within JPMorgan Private Bank account statements. An EFTA page/accession can contain several transaction rows. The record must be classified by its actual document type and row content, not by the report's table label.

The DOJ-hosted PDF locator surfaced in public search for EFTA01482909 identifies a JPMorgan Private Bank primary-account statement for Jeffrey E. Epstein for February 1–29, 2008, and the reproduced OCR shows multiple transfer rows on that statement page. The report's "from DDA" designation is therefore a transaction-row description, not proof that the EFTA PDF itself is a standalone transfer advice.

## 11. Independent source reliability and interpretation correction

The inter-entity report is explicitly labeled AI-generated and warns that it may contain errors. Its narrative goes beyond the available indexed excerpts in places, including asserting that:
- the entire roster was one commingled fund pool;
- all accounts were treated as one client relationship for all purposes;
- a named asset account definitively belonged to Southern Trust and was liquidated into Epstein's personal account;
- transfers establish a specific entity network or client-directed control.

Those propositions require original document context and should not be carried into the claim ledger as established facts based only on the report. The DOJ production format memorandum explains that the Department produced records as formatted upon entry into its review system and that EFTA Bates numbering is not proof of the contents' truth. Use the original documents to establish what the bank recorded, and separate bank-recorded descriptions from independently established ownership and settlement.

DOJ production-format memorandum: https://www.justice.gov/opa/media/1426091/dl?inline=
