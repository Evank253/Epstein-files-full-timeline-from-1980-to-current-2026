## Correction receipt — scope of the $1,000 / $100,000 reference

The prior summary incorrectly characterized the $1,000 and $100,000 figures as Bear's review thresholds and described a Bear conclusion that no suspicious wires were found. Re-examination of the source shows the relevant filing is USVI Statement of Material Facts, S.D.N.Y. Document 246, filed July 26, 2023, paragraphs 171–177. Paragraph 174 quotes a January 10, 2011 JPMorgan email recalling that the AUSA request concerned “something about $1000 and $100,000 transactions.” This is a staff member's recollection of the request, not evidence of Bear's actual review methodology or result. The underlying Exhibit 125 (JPM-SDNYLIT-00373074–00373076) is designated sealed; Exhibit 127 (JPM-SDNYLIT-00157192–00157193) has not been acquired here. Earlier claims that Bear screened those amounts and found no suspicious wires are withdrawn. No transaction-level conclusion is made. See corrected EVT-044/045, EVD-022, CLM-023, and ART-024.
