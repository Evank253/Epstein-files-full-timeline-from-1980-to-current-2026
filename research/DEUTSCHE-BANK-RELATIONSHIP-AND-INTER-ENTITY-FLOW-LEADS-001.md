## 8. Interpretation rules

- Bank balance ≠ income, transaction or total wealth.
- Account relationship grouping ≠ beneficial ownership or commingling.
- Banker assignment ≠ approval or knowledge of every transaction.
- KYC representation ≠ independently verified beneficial ownership.
- AML inquiry ≠ finding of criminality or proof a SAR was filed.
- OCR/classifier feature ≠ verified document content.
- AI-generated analytical report ≠ primary evidence.

The independent database identifies its analytical reports as AI-generated and warns that errors may remain. Use them only to locate records. DOJ originals remain the source of record. DOJ itself warns that some material may not be electronically searchable and that sensitive information may inadvertently appear in the public library.
