# Epstein Files — Full Timeline from 1980 to Current (2026)

Dedicated repository for the public-record investigation. MANIFEX, MANIFEX ART, Genesis Seed, Seed Bank, the 72-agent swarm, MiroFish and other capabilities are analytical tools used within this chat only. They are not investigation-artifact destinations and must not be modified.

## Evidence standards
- Public, lawfully accessible records only.
- Preserve original records and provenance; a URL is not a captured file.
- Every extracted claim, event and relationship must link to a source artifact and exact page, line, docket, timestamp or record locator where available.
- Distinguish court findings, regulator findings, official statements, attributed testimony, allegations, reporting and unresolved claims.
- A person's appearance in a document or graph is not evidence of wrongdoing.
- Do not publish victim or witness identifying information, intimate imagery, illegal sexual-abuse material or sensitive personal details.
- Do not infer guilt, intent, intelligence affiliation or criminal participation from association, graph proximity or co-occurrence.
- Retain duplicate source copies and connect them through duplicate-cluster records; do not delete originals.
