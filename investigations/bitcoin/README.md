## Evidence rules

1. Official DOJ and House Oversight records are primary source families, but the original file/page must be acquired and inspected before marking it image-verified.
2. Independent OCR/indexes are discovery aids, not substitutes for official source files.
3. News reporting is secondary evidence; attribute its claims and seek the underlying records.
4. Distinguish Epstein's own donations, investments through controlled entities, fund-level investments, other limited partners' capital, and investments made by unrelated third parties.
5. A fund portfolio schedule does not by itself prove that Epstein personally funded every portfolio company or controlled the fund's investment decisions.
6. Distinguish funding of an academic initiative from authorship, protocol design, code contributions, governance authority, or control of Bitcoin.
7. No association, investment, email, or funding relationship proves criminal conduct, secret coordination, or authorship of Bitcoin.
8. Preserve conflicting amounts and classifications as separate leads until reconciled against signed subscription agreements, capital calls, bank records, ledgers, and cap tables.
9. Do not reproduce victim-identifying or sensitive personal information.
